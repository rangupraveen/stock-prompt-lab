About the framework

What is a multi-bagger?

A multi-bagger is a stock that returns a multiple of its original price — 2x, 5x, 10x or more. The term comes from baseball, where each "bag" is a base. Finding them requires looking beyond surface-level metrics to understand durable competitive advantages, massive addressable markets, and management with skin in the game.

Why use a structured prompt?

Most investors ask AI vague questions and get vague answers. This framework forces the AI to think like a fund manager — evaluating moat durability, operating leverage, insider ownership, and realistic valuation scenarios. The structure is what makes the output actually useful.

Which AI works best?

Claude and GPT-4o give the most nuanced financial reasoning. For the best results, paste the prompt directly — don't summarize it. Always cross-reference AI output with primary sources: earnings calls, 10-Ks, and analyst reports.

Not financial advice

This tool generates research prompts — not buy or sell recommendations. All analysis produced by AI models should be treated as a starting point for your own due diligence. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results. Invest only what you can afford to lose.
